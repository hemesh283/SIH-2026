### Q2. What are the exact accelerometer columns?
In the smartphone files (`S-*.csv`):
- ` ACCELEROMETER X (m/s²) `
- ` ACCELEROMETER Y (m/s²)`
- ` ACCELEROMETER Z (m/s²)`
- Accompanying Gravity channels:
  - ` GRAVITY X (m/s²)`
  - ` GRAVITY Y (m/s²)`
  - ` GRAVITY Z (m/s²)`
- Linear acceleration (gravity-compensated) is derived as:
  $$\mathbf{a}_{\text{linear}} = \mathbf{a}_{\text{accel}} - \mathbf{g}_{\text{gravity}}$$

### Q4. What are their units?
- **Accelerometer & Gravity:** $\text{m/s}^2$ (converted to $g$ via $/ 9.80665$ to match model input contract).
- **Gyroscope:** $\text{rad/s}$.
- **Timestamps:** Milliseconds (ms).
- **GPS Speed:** $\text{km/h}$ (converted to $\text{m/s}$ via $/ 3.6$).
- **GPS Coordinates:** Decimal degrees WGS-84.
- **Orientation / Heading:** Degrees $[0, 360)$.

### Q5. What is the best synchronized vehicle/reference ground truth?
- **Selected Ground Truth:** **Vehicle ECU dataset (`V-*.csv`)**.
- **Key Columns:**
  - ` Latitude (degrees)`
  - ` Longitude (degrees)`
  - ` Velocity (km/hr)`
  - ` Heading (degrees)`
- **Justification:** Vehicle CAN data is generated by an external roof-mounted receiver coupled with 4-wheel independent rotational speed odometry (`Wheel Speed Front Left`, `Front Right`, `Rear Left`, `Rear Right` in $\text{rad/s}$), steering angle, and chassis yaw rate sensors, whereas smartphone GPS suffers from multipath and lower chip precision.

### Q10. What window length gives us a sensible physical time horizon?

At the native **10.0 Hz** sampling frequency of IO-VNBD:

| Candidate Window ($W$) | Sampling Rate | Physical Duration ($T$) | Mean Vehicle Distance Traversed ($\bar{v} \cdot T$) | Physical & ML Rationale |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **20 samples** | **10 Hz** | **2.0 seconds** | **~25.0 meters** | **(Recommended)** Preserves the exact same physical time horizon ($2.0\text{ s}$) as OxIOD ($200\text{ samples} \times 0.01\text{ s} = 2.0\text{ s}$). A 2-second horizon maintains near-linear displacement assumption during cornering. Stride = 10 samples ($1.0\text{ s}$). |
| **50 samples** | 10 Hz | 5.0 seconds | ~62.5 meters | Covers ~60 meters; turns introduce noticeable path curvature within the window. |
| **100 samples** | 10 Hz | 10.0 seconds | ~125.0 meters | Multiple turns or stop-and-go actions within a single window increase variance. |
| **200 samples** | 10 Hz | 20.0 seconds | ~250.0 meters | Over 20 seconds, road geometry and traffic cause multi-modal paths; displacement vector $[dx, dy, dz]$ becomes decoupled from current IMU state. |
| **200 samples (Resampled to 100 Hz)** | 100 Hz (interpolated) | 2.0 seconds | ~25.0 meters | Preserves the exact `[batch, 200, 6]` tensor shape and weight compatibility of the existing Android ONNX session, but uses linear interpolation between 10 Hz samples. |
